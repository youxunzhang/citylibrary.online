Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about library services, membership, and resources.

How do I borrow books from the library?

Simply bring your library card to any location, browse our catalog, and check out up to 20 items for 3 weeks. You can also place holds online and pick them up when ready.

How much does a library card cost?

Library cards are free for city residents. Non-residents may be charged a small annual fee. Children under 18 can get a free card with parent/guardian permission.

Can I access e-books and audiobooks online?

Yes! With your library card, you can access thousands of e-books and audiobooks through our digital library. Download the mobile app or visit our website to get started.

How do I renew my borrowed items?

You can renew items online through your account, via our mobile app, by phone, or in person. Most items can be renewed up to 3 times if no one else has placed a hold.

What if I lose my library card?

Report a lost card immediately by calling us or visiting any location. There's a small replacement fee. We can also help you access your account with photo ID.

Do you offer volunteer opportunities?

Yes! We welcome volunteers for various programs including children's story time, book sorting, event assistance, and community outreach. Contact us for more information.

Can I reserve study rooms online?

Yes, you can reserve study rooms and meeting spaces through our online reservation system. Rooms can be booked up to 2 weeks in advance for 2-hour sessions.

What databases do you offer?

We provide access to academic databases, business resources, genealogy tools, legal databases, and more. All are available free with your library card, both in-person and remotely.
